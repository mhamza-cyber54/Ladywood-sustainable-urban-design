# Ladywood Sustainable Urban Design Project

A university group project completed at Aston University focused on developing a sustainable urban design proposal for the Ladywood area of Birmingham.

## Project Overview

The project involved identifying challenges affecting the Ladywood area and developing a practical urban design proposal that considered social, environmental and economic factors.

Our group developed a sustainable pavement concept designed to contribute towards a safer, more accessible and environmentally responsible urban environment.

## My Contribution

As a member of the project team, I contributed to the development and communication of the proposed solution. My work included:

- Contributing ideas and helping develop the proposed solution to the identified problem.
- Researching and considering social, environmental and economic factors affecting the proposal.
- Contributing to the design and production of the final project poster.
- Organising technical and research information into a clear visual presentation.
- Working collaboratively with team members to develop and refine the final proposal.
- Presenting the project and its proposed solution in front of the class and explaining our work.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Problem solving and solution development
- Research and critical thinking
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Written and visual communication
- Poster design and information presentation
- Public speaking and presentation
- Sustainability awareness
- Project planning

## Project Output

The final outcome was a group poster presenting the sustainable pavement proposal, including the problem context, justification for the proposed solution, design considerations and reflection on the project.
